The Atelier Heritage & Charter

Reinventing Luxury Through Living Bark

Founded on the premise that true luxury requires neither animal harm nor petroleum polymers, Cork Pochette bridges centuries-old European maroquinerie disciplines with the miraculous material science of Mediterranean cork oak forests.

1. The Sanctuary of the Montado Woodland

The story of our pochettes begins in the sun-drenched cork oak savannas (montados) of the Portuguese Alentejo. Unlike timber forestry where trees are felled, cork harvesting is a gentle surgical art performed exclusively during the warmest summer months. Highly skilled descortiçadores use specialized curved axes to unpeel the outer bark without ever nicking the inner cambium layer. The tree remains alive, thriving for over two hundred years while regenerating a fresh, thicker mantle of bark every nine to ten years.

2. Bench Craftsmanship at 181 Mercer Street

In our Manhattan atelier, each sheet of raw cork textile is inspected by hand under natural north-facing daylight. Every pattern piece is hand-cut with razor-sharp Japanese skiving knives, reinforced with natural unbleached cotton twill, and pricked using French irons at eight stitches per inch. Two hand needles loaded with waxed linen thread bind each perimeter seam in a saddle stitch that withstands decades of daily use.
