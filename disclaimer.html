Artisanal Craft & Material Disclaimer

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Certified Document ID: 181-MERCER-LEGAL-CORK

1. Artisanal Information and Craft Scope

The technical monographs, suberin polymer analyses, hand saddle-stitching guides, and material benchmark tables published across corkpochette.com are prepared exclusively for educational, artistic, and collector reference purposes. While all material density specifications, tensile test metrics, and historical silviculture descriptions are researched with meticulous historical and scientific accuracy, content on this domain does not constitute certified chemical engineering advice or formal commercial testing certifications. Individuals utilizing specialized leatherworking awls, knives, or burnishing solvents must observe certified workshop safety measures at all times.

2. Natural Material Wear and Care Cautions

While Quercus suber cork textile is naturally hydrophobic, lightweight, and durable, it remains an organic plant-based material that requires reasonable care and respectful handling. Exposure to open flames, sharp abrasive edges, concentrated industrial solvents, or heavy cosmetic staining agents may permanently disfigure the natural suberin surface. Cork Pochette Atelier disclaims all legal responsibility for aesthetic degradation or physical wear resulting from improper storage, neglected maintenance, or exposure to harsh environmental conditions beyond intended evening accessory use.

3. Depiction of Natural Colors and Screen Variations

We photograph all bespoke pochettes and cork bark swatches in professional studio environments under color-calibrated fifty-five hundred kelvin daylight simulation lighting. However, because individual smartphone screens, computer monitors, and digital display panels exhibit varying color reproduction standards, contrast calibrations, and ambient brightness levels, physical cork tones may vary subtly from screen renderings. We encourage prospective patrons to schedule private salon consultations at our 181 Mercer Street studio to view physical textile swatches prior to finalizing commissions.

4. Bespoke Lead Times and Supply Integrity

Stated production timeframes for bespoke commissions represent conscientious professional estimates based upon current workbench capacity and seasonal cork harvest availability in Portugal. Unforeseen delays arising from severe meteorological events in European woodlands, specialized metal foundry delays, or complex custom hardware casting may occasionally extend anticipated completion dates. Cork Pochette Atelier disclaims liability for secondary scheduling inconveniences resulting from minor craftsmanship delays necessary to preserve our uncompromising standard of handcrafted luxury.

5. External Academic Citations and References

Our technical monographs frequently cite historical forestry studies, European guild publications, and botanical research papers authored by third-party academic institutions. These scholarly citations and hyperlinks are included solely for intellectual depth, educational enrichment, and collector convenience. Cork Pochette Atelier does not exercise editorial control over external third-party servers, nor does it imply official corporate endorsement, partnership, or liability for information maintained on external academic or environmental websites.
