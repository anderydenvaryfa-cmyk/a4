Maroquinerie Terms & Covenants

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Certified Document ID: 181-MERCER-LEGAL-CORK

1. Contractual Governance and Atelier Scope

By accessing corkpochette.com or commissioning custom artisanal provisions from our workshop, patrons formally enter into an enforceable covenant governed by federal and state commercial statutes. All structural maroquinerie specifications, technical cork suberin treatises, and archival design patterns published across our digital domain are the exclusive intellectual property of Cork Pochette Atelier. Unauthorized programmatic scraping, commercial reproduction, or secondary exploitation of our bespoke bag patterns and written craft monographs is strictly prohibited under international copyright conventions and intellectual property laws worldwide.

2. Bespoke Commission Protocol and Deposits

Every custom handcrafted evening pochette or monogrammed clutch requires formal written design validation and a non-refundable initial artisan retainer before bench fabrication commences. Because bespoke commissions entail individualized pattern drafting, rare Portuguese cork textile allocation, and extensive hand saddle-stitching hours by master leathercrafters, custom commissions cannot be cancelled once leather cutting begins. Patrons receive scheduled milestone updates and high-resolution studio photographs as their commissioned piece progresses through our 181 Mercer Street workshop.

3. Craftsmanship Standards and Lifetime Seam Warranty

We warrant every handcrafted cork pochette against material structural failure, seam separation, and hardware malfunction under normal collector usage for the lifetime of the original owner. Because hand saddle-stitching with French waxed linen thread creates an exceptionally resilient locked seam, we guarantee structural integrity and provide complimentary bench re-stitching should a thread ever fail. Damages resulting from sharp puncture lacerations, chemical solvent exposure, or unauthorized third-party alterations are explicitly excluded from warranty coverage and repair indemnification.

4. Limitation of Atelier Operational Liability

Cork Pochette Atelier, its resident master artisans, and corporate trustees shall not be held liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages arising from digital platform downtime, electronic transmission delays, or municipal postal courier disruptions. Our maximum legal financial liability relating to any commissioned piece or service transaction shall not exceed the verified transactional amount paid directly to our atelier by the patron. We maintain comprehensive commercial liability insurance and fully insured, signature-required transit protocols across all domestic and international shipments.

5. Handcrafted Natural Material Variation

Natural cork leather derived from the bark of Quercus suber is an organic, unmanufactured botanical material displaying subtle grain variations, natural tonal gradations, and tactile surface characteristics unique to each individual tree harvest. Patrons acknowledge that minor surface fissures, lenticel distribution patterns, and organic color shading are authentic hallmarks of genuine botanical craftsmanship rather than manufacturing defects. While our artisans strive to match client preferences precisely, each finished pochette remains a distinct, irreproducible natural work of art.

6. Administrative Covenants and Guild Governance

Our executive atelier management reserves the unilateral operational authority to update, revise, or amend these institutional terms without prior individualized notice to past visitors or patrons. Updated covenants become legally enforceable immediately upon publication to this official digital registry. For formal contractual inquiries or legal verification of institutional standing, write to our headquarters at 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States, or telephone our administrative desk at +1-888-777-5845 for immediate registrar assistance.
