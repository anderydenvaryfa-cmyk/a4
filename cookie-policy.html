Cookie & Telemetry Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Certified Document ID: 181-MERCER-LEGAL-CORK

1. Educational Policy Statement and Purpose

This Cookie and Digital Telemetry Policy outlines how Cork Pochette Atelier employs minimal, privacy-respecting technologies across corkpochette.com to deliver an elegant, uninterrupted digital salon experience. As a dedicated artisanal luxury house, we reject intrusive third-party commercial tracking, behavioral user profiling, and aggressive programmatic advertising trackers. Our digital infrastructure deploys cookies exclusively to sustain essential site navigation, maintain secure consultation request sessions, and record user accessibility selections throughout our online portfolio.

2. Essential Operational and Security Tokens

Essential cookies are small, secure cryptographic data strings stored locally on your browsing terminal to enable seamless transitions between pages, prevent cross-site request forgery, and maintain active bespoke consultation form data. These technical mechanisms are strictly functional in nature; without them, dynamic features such as our interactive commission calculator, appointment scheduling widgets, and protected contact portals cannot operate reliably. Essential session tokens expire automatically once you close your active browser session or complete form submission.

3. Aggregated Performance Telemetry and Analytics

To understand which bespoke pochette models, suberin treatises, and craftsmanship monographs generate the greatest interest among discerning collectors, our servers record anonymous, aggregated browsing telemetry. These metrics measure page loading intervals, server response benchmarks, and regional traffic volumes without linking operational statistics to personally identifiable client records. Analytical data is reviewed solely by our internal design team to resolve technical bottlenecks, enhance mobile navigation, and optimize our digital monograph library.

4. Absolute Exclusion of Advertising Retargeting

Cork Pochette Atelier maintains a strict, permanent policy prohibiting third-party advertising cookies, retargeting pixels, and commercial data-broker tracking beacons across all hosted URLs. We do not participate in commercial programmatic ad exchanges, behavioral monetization syndicates, or cross-device profile harvesting networks. Your exploration of our bespoke maroquinerie portfolio remains completely private and unmonitored by commercial marketing conglomerates, ensuring an uncompromised environment dedicated purely to craftsmanship and timeless design.

5. Client Configuration and Browser Controls

Patrons and web visitors maintain complete statutory autonomy to configure their individual browser environments to accept, block, or delete technical cookies at their discretion. Most modern desktop and mobile browsers provide dedicated privacy settings allowing users to clear browsing caches, restrict third-party scripts, or navigate in private browsing windows. Please be advised that disabling essential technical cookies may prevent proper functionality of our interactive bespoke pochette customizer and online salon booking tools.

6. Institutional Telemetry Registry and Contact

If you require further regulatory clarification regarding our digital telemetry practices, wish to inspect our technical cookie registry, or need assistance managing privacy configurations, please contact our information security team. Inquiries may be directed by mail to Cork Pochette Atelier, 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States, or by telephoning our administrative office at +1-888-777-5845 during standard business hours for prompt technical assistance.
