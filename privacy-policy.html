Institutional Governance & Legal Standards

Institutional Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Certified Document ID: 181-MERCER-LEGAL-CORK

1. Institutional Scope and Client Privacy Commitment

At Cork Pochette Atelier, accessible through corkpochette.com, safeguarding the absolute confidentiality, personal privacy, and security of all bespoke clients and collectors is our primary operational covenant. We maintain rigorous physical, electronic, and administrative safeguards to protect your personal identity, contact coordinates, and bespoke commission specifications against unauthorized access, loss, compromise, or unlawful distribution. Under no circumstances do we lease, monetize, or disclose client identity dossiers to external commercial advertising networks, data brokers, or marketing syndicates across any jurisdiction worldwide.

2. Collection of Commission Records and Telemetry

When scheduling private salon consultations at our Mercer Street workshop, ordering bespoke evening clutches, or inquiring about custom monogramming, we gather essential contact information including your full legal name, telephone number, physical delivery address, and electronic mail coordinates. This confidential data allows our master artisans to coordinate private appointments, discuss customized leathercraft options, and maintain accurate lifetime provenance records for every finished piece. Furthermore, our web hosting servers automatically record standard network telemetry, such as browser types, IP addresses, and page access timestamps to guarantee portal security and peak digital performance.

3. Essential Session Cookies and Browsing Security

Our digital platform uses strictly necessary session cookies designed to sustain secure navigation, maintain active consultation forms, and preserve user accessibility preferences across our online atelier. These digital tokens operate purely within our primary domain and do not track your external browsing habits, monitor third-party web visits, or assemble commercial behavioral dossiers. Clients may configure their individual browser settings to reject non-essential cookies at any time, although doing so may temporarily impair interactive features such as our bespoke commission calculator and online salon scheduling tools.

4. Authorized Personnel Access and Non-Disclosure

Client commission details and transaction records are accessed solely by authorized atelier concierges, master maroquiniers, and secure infrastructure engineers bound by comprehensive confidentiality agreements. We do not disclose personal collector portfolios to third parties, except where strictly required to complete insured courier delivery transit or satisfy mandatory statutory compliance obligations under federal and state commercial laws. Any external payment gateways or freight carriers contracted by our atelier must demonstrably adhere to industry-standard data encryption benchmarks and strict consumer protection standards.

5. Secure Data Retention and Deletion Rights

We preserve private transaction ledgers, bespoke design specifications, and consultation communications exclusively for durations required to fulfill active maroquinerie commissions, satisfy tax documentation rules, and support our lifetime craftsmanship warranty registry. Upon the conclusion of statutory retention periods, identifiable digital records are permanently purged from our encrypted server infrastructure. Collectors maintain full statutory entitlements to request a certified extract of their stored personal records, request immediate data corrections, or seek permanent erasure from our non-essential marketing archives at any time.

6. Institutional Privacy Officer Contact Registry

Collectors and visitors seeking legal clarification regarding our privacy protocols, wishing to exercise statutory data privileges, or requiring administrative assistance may contact our privacy compliance desk directly. Formal correspondence may be addressed by post to Cork Pochette Atelier, 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States, or submitted electronically via our verified concierge email. Alternatively, patrons may telephone our direct office line at +1-888-777-5845 during standard atelier business hours for prompt personal assistance.
